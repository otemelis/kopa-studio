A denser cool-neutral workstation. The watchlist sits beside the chart on wider displays for repeated row-by-row inspection. The same analytical state and source rules apply.

Five-minute interaction review

Follow a movement from history to a decision.

  1. Open Keywords. Select a third keyword in the table, then toggle one off in the legend.
  2. Scrub a date with the slider or arrow keys. Read current and previous observation dates, method and missing states.
  3. Use Select range to analyze the last week. Inspect the new comparator and coverage, then clear the range.
  4. Open a keyword and a dated chart event. Try Explain this change and the prefilled experiment-note form.
  5. Open Apple Ads. Drill into a campaign, ad group, targeting keywords and reported terms. Switch attribution, then review delay and ambiguity.
