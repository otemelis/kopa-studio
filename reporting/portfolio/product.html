Definitions and limitations

POIS active installations: distinct installation_id with any event in the selected environment and UTC period. Activation card: installations starting any posture session; first-session completion is shown separately in the ordered journey. Purchase completions are client observations; trial and explicit non-trial signals remain separate and neither is verified revenue.

The closed journey starts at the first onboarding_started in the period, uses one installation per entry and requires sequential stages within 24 hours. Entries without a complete horizon before period end are excluded. Completion joins the same posture session ID. Missing later events mean not observed within the horizon, not proven abandonment.

Retention cohorts use each installation’s earliest recorded first_launch=true in that environment, restricted to the selected window. D1/D7 require the entire target UTC day before period end. A return is app_opened, app_foregrounded or session_started. Reinstalls, telemetry gaps and consent affect coverage.

PASSPORT records anonymous aggregate counts on device-local dates. Distinct installations, linked journeys and retention are unavailable. Retry duplication and upload-time channel/version stamping remain known limitations. Source failure never substitutes sample data.

All counts describe received telemetry, not all customers. Today is excluded; delayed events can revise prior days. No App Store production activity is inferred from TestFlight.
