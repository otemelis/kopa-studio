Reconciliation and source definitions

Every campaign sum matches the CSV footer. The footer is excluded from campaign rows. Spend uses the original EUR amounts; CPA is spend divided by total attributed installs, and is unavailable with no attributed installs. Total installs are not added to tap-through installs: the latter is a component of the total.

11 October: the stored active campaign matches the export numerically (13 impressions, no spend, taps or attributed installs). Stored account timezone was not persisted, so its original timezone provenance remains unverified. The export explicitly reports UTC. Missing stored rows for the other two campaigns remain missing, while their official exported zero values are valid.

11–14 October: the database has only 11 October and a partial 12 October; 13–14 October are absent. This range cannot be reconciled against that incomplete history or split into daily metrics. No differences are forced to match. Zero-install examples do not validate attribution mapping for nonzero outcomes.

POIS, PASSPORT and LoveType are not present in these supplied reports. No zero activity is inferred for them. Campaign statuses describe the export, not current delivery. No bids, budgets or campaigns were changed.
