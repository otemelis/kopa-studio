Why do I see ads?+

2048 Run Merge is free to play with no purchases. Ads help fund the game. Most are optional rewards—revive after a crash, boost at the wall, or double your coins—plus an occasional ad between levels.

How do I get more coins?+

Collect coins during a run, claim your daily streak bonus, and use the optional Double Coins reward after a win.

I lost progress. Can it be restored?+

Progress, coins, upgrades, and best scores are saved only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so reinstalling or deleting the app permanently resets them.

An ad or reward did not load.+

Ad availability can occasionally vary. Try again in a moment; if a reward still does not arrive, email us with the device and what happened.

How do I manage ad tracking?+

On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining tracking keeps the game fully playable and uses non-personalized ads.
