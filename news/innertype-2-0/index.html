InnerType 2.0 is live

InnerType 2.0 is out today. The biggest change is under the hood: the Communication Style test has been rebuilt from the ground up on newer conflict-style research, with the same six-minute runtime but noticeably sharper results.

Onboarding is shorter too — most people now reach their first result in under a minute of setup.

The synthesis report, the piece that ties all four dimensions together once you’ve completed at least one assessment, has been rewritten to read less like a clinical printout and more like something a thoughtful friend would say back to you.

As always, your answers and results stay on-device — no account required. InnerType collects only limited, non-identifying usage analytics to improve the app; your answers and reports are never sold, and nothing you enter leaves your phone unless you choose to share a result card.
