Douze · Jan 18, 2026

A first look at Douze

Alongside Douze Draft, we’re building a second Eurovision-related app, currently just called Douze — the French word for twelve, and the top score a country can award another in Eurovision’s voting system.

It’s early enough that we’re not ready to describe what it does yet. It’s running in its own environment, separate from Draft, while we figure out which parts of the idea are worth keeping.

We’ll post here when there’s something real to show.
