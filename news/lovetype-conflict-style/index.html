LoveType adds the Conflict Style test

LoveType launched with two tests — Attachment Style and Love Style — and a promise that a third was coming. That third ring is here.

Conflict Style looks at what you actually do when things get tense: whether you lean in, go quiet, need space, or want to talk it out immediately. Combined with your Attachment and Love Style results, it completes your full LoveType profile — ten possible archetypes, built entirely from your own answers.

The test takes about four minutes. Existing users will see their profile update automatically once they complete it; nothing is lost from tests you’ve already taken.
