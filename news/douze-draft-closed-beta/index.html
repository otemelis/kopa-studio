Douze Draft opens a small closed beta

Douze Draft — our first Eurovision-related app — has entered a small closed beta. It’s early. The name gives away the idea: a place to draft your predictions before a contest, then see how the actual voting compares.

We’re keeping the group small on purpose. Eurovision’s format, rules, and voting structure shift enough between years that we’d rather get the fundamentals right with a handful of testers than rush a version that doesn’t hold up contest to contest.

No public sign-up yet. If that changes, it’ll show up here first.
